Many real problems look more like Brass than like chess: several actors share limited resources, borrow against the future and build networks whose value appears much later. A fast, verified and open environment for this kind of planning is useful to multi-agent research. And a complete pipeline built without paid compute shows that serious reinforcement-learning research is not reserved for large labs.
