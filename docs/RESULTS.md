# BrassZero results

As of 20 November 2026, one score-reward training variant won 32 of 32 games against three rule-based Greedy opponents. The same eight deals rotated the model through all four seats. This comparison uses one training replicate.

The largest score in the archived snapshot was 124 VP, achieved by experiment update 843 in an evaluation game against three AI players. It is a single-game maximum. Historical chart values describe a mixture of training, evaluation and other recordings. Human playing strength remains unmeasured.

The main-line working model at the stated date was BC 788, and its latest main-line checkpoint was 793. The historical school and its model selection are separate. The combination label BC denotes the combined critic and score-reward treatments.

To inspect the downloadable chart and its recorded sample sizes run `make site-data`. The archive projection and per-model evaluation evidence are described in the datasheet and model cards.
