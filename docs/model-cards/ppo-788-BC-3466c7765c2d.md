## Use and training

This policy plays four-player Brass: Birmingham from native observations and public events. It was trained with PPO against itself and a league of opponents. Intended uses include gameplay, evaluation, and research. The branch played 100 training games during the paired training study. Earlier games belong to the inherited parent lineage. Each training lineup was drawn with probability 50% for self-play in all four seats, 35% for one learning seat against three frozen snapshots, and 15% for one learning seat against three opponents drawn from the snapshots and the rule-based Random and Greedy players. Greedy is therefore also a training opponent. Compared with its parent run, this branch changed `ppo.critic_target` from `gae` to `terminal`, `ppo.reward` from `strict_win` to `score`. `BC` is the study's label for these two changes. It does not mean behaviour cloning. The [model repository](https://huggingface.co/brasszero/brasszero-ppo) describes the full training line.
